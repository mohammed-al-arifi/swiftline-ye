LOGO PLACEMENT INSTRUCTIONS
============================

Place the Swift Line company logo in this folder as:
  logo.png

Requirements:
- Format: PNG (transparent background preferred)
- Minimum size: 320 × 112 px (any proportional size)
- The header displays it at 160px wide × auto height
- For best results, use a wide logo (wider than tall)

Additionally, you can place:
  og-image.jpg  — Social media preview image (1200 × 630 px recommended)

FAVICON INSTRUCTIONS
====================

Generate favicons from the logo using:
  https://favicon.io
  https://realfavicongenerator.net

Place the generated files in the /favicon/ folder:
  favicon.ico
  favicon-32x32.png
  favicon-16x16.png
  apple-touch-icon.png
